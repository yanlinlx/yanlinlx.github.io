Research experience

Generative peptide design with diffusion models

Feb. 2025 – present · principal investigator

Training a diffusion-based generative model for peptide design, extending the PepGLAD framework. I curated a much larger peptide–target complex dataset for training, fine-tuned on diverse subsets to raise the design success rate, and wired an external protein property predictor in as a constraint so that generation is steered toward peptides with real therapeutic relevance.

  • Diffusion models
  • Peptide design
  • Generative AI
  • Dataset curation

SYNBIP 2.0 — synthetic binding protein database

Jul. 2023 – Sept. 2024 · principal investigator

Led the major update of SYNBIP, the reference database for synthetic binding proteins, addressing fragmented data, inconsistent standards, missing epitope information and narrow scaffold diversity. The update added 3D structures for 899 SBP–target complexes, expanded the SBP sequence space fivefold to 12,025, and catalogued over 16 million candidate scaffolds mined from the PDB and AlphaFold DB. Built with manual literature curation, protein–protein docking, structural alignment, AI and automated web-crawling pipelines.

  • Database
  • Data curation
  • Protein–protein docking
  • Web mining
DatabasePaper

Expanding the sequence space of synthetic binding proteins

Nov. 2022 – Mar. 2023 · principal investigator

Applied the deep-learning design framework ProteinMPNN to synthetic binding proteins, sidestepping the dependence of site-directed mutagenesis on parent-protein structure. Designed 7,245 new sequences across 55 scaffolds and screened them in silico: the generated proteins showed improved solubility and thermal stability over known SBPs, and eight scaffolds turned out to be especially well suited to the method.

  • ProteinMPNN
  • De novo design
  • Deep learning
  • Solubility / stability
Paper

AI for pharmacology — industry research training

Sept. 2024 – present · China Resources Double Crane Pharmaceutical

Evaluated and deployed machine-learning models for pharmacology tasks, providing technical support to early drug-discovery pipelines. Alongside the computational work I ran cell-based assays and mouse toxicity and efficacy studies, and carried out the statistical analysis backing clinical-trial applications.
